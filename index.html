This depends on your plan, not your headcount

The saving comes from the gap between the plan you buy now and a leaner design. If that gap is small, so is the opportunity.

Worth a look

  • A genuinely good plan today, typically under a $500 deductible with low or no copays
  • Spending above roughly $9,000 to $10,000 per covered employee per year
  • Competing for staff, so cutting the plan is not an acceptable answer
  • A renewal inside the next twelve months

Probably not

  • Already on a high deductible plan, since the premium has little left to give
  • Deductibles above roughly $3,000, where the restructuring has largely happened
  • Very small headcount, where fixed administration outweighs the saving
  • A renewal that has just passed, since the next window is a year out
